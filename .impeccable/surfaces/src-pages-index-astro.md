# Home — vertikaplatforms.com

Scope: homepage (`src/pages/index.astro`); the three audience pages (`/personas`, `/empresas`, `/salud`) and inner pages inherit this world.
Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Personas, Empresas and Salud visitors evaluating an unknown Peruvian agency. Action: write to Vertika on WhatsApp (email secondary). Proof available: none external; trust comes from clarity of offer, process, team and data practices. No testimonials, metrics or client logos.

## Direction contract

Pinned by the user on 2026-11-10: use ghostcli.cloud as the visual reference (captures in referencias-web/extra-oscura/ghostcli/); replaces the earlier "Quipu de datos" contract. Brand colours and DM Sans body stay; Archivo (expanded, 860) is added for display, DM Mono for data.

THESIS: The site is a working file folder, not a brochure: every section shows the work being done (a project file, a comparison sheet, a brief that fills itself in, mini interfaces, deliverables per step). It refuses the split hero plus a row of identical icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool crumpled paper ground (Blender texture), navy ink, expanded uppercase display headlines with one phrase in Azul Vertika, a 2px ink rule plus a blue diamond opening every section, white sheets with a soft shadow, mono for data with dotted leaders, perforated edges and zigzag receipts, a hand-drawn blue underline or circle as the only annotation. Turquoise only as knots and ticks. 3D Blender objects (V symbol, three audience objects, quipu) in the same palette.

STORY: Visitors see what Vertika builds, choose their audience, see a sample project file advance, compare the three project types, build a brief and send it by WhatsApp, see what they get at each step, how data is protected, and who is behind it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A four-line display headline on the left with "a medida." in blue, the lead, audience chips, then a navy WhatsApp button and an "Arma tu brief" outline button. On the right, a perforated project-file sheet in mono for the chosen audience, a hand-drawn "Todo por escrito" note, and that audience's 3D object overlapping the sheet's corner. Below: a four-fact sheet and a technology marquee.

FORM: Brief-pinned reference (ghostcli.cloud), adapted; seed:fff26cd154598676 (original roll, superseded by the pin). Signature interaction: the audience chips switch the project file, the 3D object, the WhatsApp message and the brief builder; the brief builder writes a live receipt and a prefilled WhatsApp message.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
