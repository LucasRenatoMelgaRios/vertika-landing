# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output). SEO is a top priority: semantic HTML, per-page metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, Open Graph/Twitter cards, JSON-LD structured data, fast Core Web Vitals. Production domain: https://vertikaplatforms.com (hosting not yet decided).

## Users

Three visitor groups, all served by one site with distinct entries (not three sites):

- **Personas**: individuals who need custom software (an app, a website, a tool for their own project or small business).
- **Empresas**: companies that need custom software, internal systems, integrations or digitalization of processes.
- **Salud**: clinics, health practices and health organizations that need software handling sensitive personal health data.

Primary market: Peru (Spanish-speaking visitors).

## Product Purpose

Vertika Platforms is a custom software development agency based in Peru. The website exists to explain clearly what Vertika builds for each audience, how it works, and how it handles data, and to get a qualified visitor to contact the team. Success = a visitor from any of the three groups understands the offer and reaches out by WhatsApp or email.

## Positioning

Custom software built from Peru, with a clear, transparent work process and deliberate care for data protection, including sensitive health data. Undecided: further differentiators beyond process clarity and data handling.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate an agency they don't know yet. They compare offers, look for the team behind it, the process, and signs that their data and investment are safe. Health-sector visitors additionally look for confidentiality and compliance with Peru's personal data protection law (Ley N.° 29733).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact channels: WhatsApp and email (business number and address to be provided by the user; kept in one config value).
- No backend, no forms posting to a server for now.
- Team section: real names, roles and photos will be supplied by the user; structure ready, content pending.
- Language: Spanish.
- Undecided: specific service catalogue per audience, pricing (not shown), technologies list (to confirm with the team), hosting provider.

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand kit and guide (kit-de-marca/).
- Visual references gathered for inspiration (referencias-web/README.md); internal only, nothing copied.
- **No clients, case studies, testimonials, metrics, reviews or client logos exist yet.** Never fabricate any of them. Trust must come from the team, the clarity of the offer, the process and data handling.

## Product Principles

1. Earn trust with truth: show process, people and data practices instead of borrowed proof.
2. One site, three clear doors: each audience finds its entry fast without the site feeling fragmented.
3. One primary action everywhere: contacting the team is always one step away.
4. Only promise what we do: security and compliance statements must be accurate.
5. Findable: content and structure are built for search from the start.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the target (contrast respecting the turquoise rule, keyboard navigation, readable on mobile).
